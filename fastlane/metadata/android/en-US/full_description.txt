UltimateTerminal is a terminal for Android for people who look after servers from a phone or a tablet. It is inspired by Termux: a newer interface that is meant to fill the whole screen on tablets, and full Linux distributions that run through proot, like WSL on Windows. Terminal only: there is no graphical environment.

<b>Early pre-release.</b> Much of the app has only been tested on a computer. It has been tried by hand on one phone (Android 17) and, for window resizing only, on one tablet (Android 12). The list below says what is in the code; the README says what has been verified on a device.

<b>What it does</b>
• Installs Debian, Ubuntu, Alpine Linux or Fedora from their official mirrors, checking the download with SHA-256, and opens it in a tab. apk, dnf, ssh, nmap and python work inside. Alpine and Fedora have been tried on a device; Debian and Ubuntu are tested only on a computer
• Works as root or as a user of your choice, which is created for you if the distro does not have it
• Several tabs and split panes, profiles (distro, user, start-up command), saved pane layouts, typing in several panes at once with a red indicator, and shortcuts you can change
• A row of extra keys (Esc, Tab, Ctrl, Alt, arrows) that shows while the keyboard is up, hardware-keyboard shortcuts and pinch zoom
• A terminal that resizes with the window, the keyboard and rotation (checked on a phone and a tablet, including a resized window), and with split screen (not checked on a device)
• Saved SSH hosts, and SSH keys you can generate, import and export; the keys are encrypted with the Android Keystore
• Optional access to the device storage from the distro, under ~/storage, for example to copy logs to Downloads. The permission is asked only when you turn it on
• Backups of one distribution, of the settings, or of everything, optionally encrypted with a password, to restore on another device
• Appearance: colour schemes, a pure-black OLED mode, a bundled monospace font or your own font file, and three styles for the extra keys
• An iOS-style interface, in English and Spanish
• Accessibility: labels and actions for every gesture, large touch targets and large-font layouts, from a code review. Not yet tried with TalkBack

<b>Not there yet</b>
TalkBack and large-font checks on a device, a timed release build (start-up to prompt is 1.6 s on a debug build, a little above the 1.5 s goal), and split screen on tablets.

<b>Why it is not on Google Play</b>
To run programs from its own storage the app targets Android 9 (API 28), which Google Play no longer accepts. That is what lets distributions run without root.

<b>Free and private</b>
No ads, no tracking, no analytics, no accounts and no Google services. The only network traffic is what you start: downloading a distribution from its official mirror, and whatever you run in the terminal. Licensed under the GPL-3.0-or-later. It reuses the terminal emulator of Termux and runs distributions with PRoot; credits and licenses are in the repository. It is an independent project, not affiliated with Termux, Debian, Ubuntu or Alpine Linux.
